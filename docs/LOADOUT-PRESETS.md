# Loadout presets

Open **Match settings > Gameplay > Pickups > Loadout presets** in Arsenal,
Mayhem or All-out. Threads does not use pickups.

Presets choose the pickup pool that can spawn; they do not equip every ball.
Automatic traps and arena hazards keep their existing rules and settings.

## Built-in presets

- **Military:** firearms, explosives, flamethrower and knife.
- **Unique Weapons:** built-in weapons outside Military, including magic and sci-fi equipment.
- **Close Combat:** knife, sword, shield, orbiting flail and Buster Sword.
- **Elemental:** fire, freeze gun, water gun, lightning, earthquake and tornado.
- **Power-ups Only:** built-in power-ups without weapon pickups.

Apply a built-in, then adjust individual cards to make your own selection.

## Saved presets

Choose items in the pickup selector, open **Loadout presets**, and press
**Save selection**. Give it a name (up to 40 characters). Saved names must be
unique, ignoring case. Use the options menu to rename or delete a saved preset.
Built-ins cannot be renamed or deleted.

Tap a preset to load it into the pickup selector, then press **Done** to apply it
to the current mode. Canceling the pickup selector leaves match settings alone.
Saving a named preset persists that preset immediately, separately from whether
you later apply the selection. Arsenal, Mayhem and All-out retain independent
pickup choices.

Presets use explicit built-in names and custom skill IDs, so a custom variant
does not replace its original. Missing, disabled or no-longer-spawnable custom
skills are identified as unavailable and skipped when applying. Empty available
pools cannot be applied. Adding a new skill later does not silently add it to
an existing saved preset.

Saved presets persist locally on the current browser/device, including offline
use. They do not currently sync between devices. Storage read/write failures
are displayed; a failed read prevents overwriting the saved preset library.
