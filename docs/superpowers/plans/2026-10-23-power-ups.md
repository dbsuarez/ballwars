# Power-ups and Match Modes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add two match modes, Arsenal (weapons + 100-point life bar, no threads) and All-Out (threads are the life, weapons cut threads), with eleven seeded power-ups, while Threads mode stays bit-for-bit unchanged.

**Architecture:** A new `PowerUpSystem` (application layer) owns field power-ups, projectiles and bombs, and is stepped by `MatchSimulationController` only when `mode != MatchMode.threads`. Per-ball power-up state lives on `SimulationBall`. All randomness goes through the controller's `SeededRandom` inside steps, so replays, the look-ahead and videos stay exact. Drawing is added to `ArenaPainter` under the existing performance rules.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter/Dart, CustomPainter, flutter_test; Python (numpy) for sounds.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-power-ups-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Determinism: every random choice comes from the controller's `SeededRandom`, drawn only inside simulation steps; outcome depends only on `(contestants, seed, mode, ball size)`.
- Threads mode (`MatchMode.threads`, the default) must produce exactly today's results for every seed.
- Spawn every 5 s of game time; at most 3 power-ups on the field; spawning a 4th removes the oldest; no expiry otherwise.
- A ball holds at most one power-up; picking another replaces it; all power-ups are single use.
- Arsenal: life 100 to 0; plain bumps do no damage. All-Out: life = threads; body contact cuts threads as in Threads mode.
- Shotgun fires 10 pellets in a fan; Gatling fires 10 rounds; Pistol fires 1 round.
- Ghost: 3 s, passes through balls, cannot be hit, in All-Out its threads can't be cut and its body cuts nothing. Speed Boost: 2x for 3 s. Freeze Ray: target stops for 2 s. Bomb: explodes about 1 s after drop, hits everything nearby including its owner. Magnet: pulls the nearest field power-up at once; if none, the next to spawn.
- Old installed apps must reject new-mode links: the mode is a ball-list token (`m.arsenal`, `m.allout`); absent means Threads.
- Arsenal slow motion uses "seconds before the win" (admin seconds, else 1.5 s; off stays off). All-Out uses the admin setting as is.
- No `MaskFilter` blurs; batch line drawing; cache text layouts (CLAUDE.md "Painting performance").
- No en/em dashes in UI strings.

## Review Focus

1. A ball eliminated while holding a power-up, or while its bullets are still flying: its pending shots and its pending Magnet must not crash or keep acting after it's out (bullets in flight may still land; nothing new is fired).
2. Last two balls killed in the same step by one bomb: a winner must still be chosen (reuse the existing tie-break: most life/threads at step start, seeded coin flip).
3. Apple in All-Out with no free rim slots: it must do nothing harmful (no infinite loop, no exception), just give 0.
4. Old links: a link with no `m.` token opens as Threads exactly as before; a link with an unknown `m.` value is rejected (null), not treated as Threads.
5. The look-ahead with power-ups: `predictedFinishStep` must equal the real finishing step for Arsenal and All-Out (the step sequence must not depend on rendering or timing).

Each line has a pinning test in the owning task (marked "Review Focus").
